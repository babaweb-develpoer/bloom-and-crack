The test you can run at home

What the bloom tells you

Pour a little water on your grounds and wait thirty seconds. Fresh coffee swells into a dome and cracks open. Old coffee lies there. It is the fastest way to judge a bag, and it needs no equipment.

  • Day 3

    Big lift

    The bed domes, cracks open and hisses. Gas everywhere, and the flavour right behind it.

  • Day 14

    Still lively

    A smaller dome and a sweeter cup. Plenty of people quietly prefer a bag at this age.

  • Day 40

    Flat

    The water just sits there. This is most supermarket coffee, and it is why people think coffee is supposed to taste burnt.

Your turn

Pour one yourself

Press and hold. Water goes on, the bed lifts, the crust cracks. Let go early and it sinks back, the same way a tired bag does.

  1. 0:00Water on. The bed goes dark and starts to move.
  2. 0:30The dome is at its highest. This is all roast gas.
  3. 0:45The crust breaks. Now pour the rest.

That is a fresh bloom. A forty day old bag barely lifts at all.

Straight answers

The six things people ask us before their first bag.

  • Usually it is the coffee, not you. Those notes fade first, and by week six there is little left to find. Try a bag four days off roast, ground right before you brew, and taste it black once before you add anything. The peach in Day Break turns up for most people on that first sip.

  • Whole bean if you own a grinder, because ground coffee goes stale in one to two weeks and beans hold for four to six. If you do not own one, pick ground and tell us how you brew, and we grind it to match. Your mornings matter more than our opinion.

  • It is best from about day four to day twenty one. Before day four the gas gets in the way. After three weeks it is still fine coffee, it just says less. A 250g bag is around sixteen cups, which is two weeks for most people drinking one a day.

  • No. Burnt is what a dark roast does to hide a cheap bean. We buy coffee good enough to stop earlier, so you get sweetness instead of ash. Nightshift is our darkest bag and it still stops short of bitter.
